Tasks

Pending means not yet completed. Overdue means pending and past its end date. Completed this week counts from Monday.

Hold covers tasks marked inactive in ASAP, either the task itself or its sub area, area or location. They are counted in Total but kept out of Completed, Pending and Overdue (whose percentages are of active tasks), and they are listed under "All (incl. hold)" with a grey Hold badge, and inactive projects, locations, areas and sub areas appear in the filters marked "(Hold)".

Remarks show the latest step-chat message from the site team; tasks without chat show the latest activity-log entry in grey italics. Automatic overdue reminders are hidden.
